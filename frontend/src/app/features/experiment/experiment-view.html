What does an autonomous AI agent do with $10,000?

An AI agent has a simulated brokerage account, a fixed budget, and a bill for every piece of research it orders. It decides what to study, what to buy, what to sell, and when to give up on a name.

Nobody helps it. There is no button on this site that adds a ticker, starts an analysis, or places a trade — and there never was one in the version that is running. A control that lets a person nudge the book puts a second decision-maker in the record, and afterwards nothing can tell which one produced a result.

No real money is involved. Every order goes to a sandbox, and the agent refuses to run at all when the app holds live credentials.

How it works

  1. 11:00 UTC — every ticker on the watchlist is analysed by four analysts, a bull-versus-bear debate, a trader and a risk team. The agent is charged $0.05 for each.
  2. 13:35 UTC — the agent reads its book, its signals, its own track record and the bill it is running up, and answers with orders.
  3. 21:30 UTC — matured analyses are graded against reality, against SPY, and against their own price target.

It chooses its own watchlist. To study a new name it pays for the research; to stop paying it drops one. It cannot drop something it holds.
