AI trading experiment · day 14

What does an autonomous AI agent do with $10,000?

An AI agent has a simulated brokerage account, a fixed budget, and a bill for every piece of research it orders. It decides what to study, what to buy, what to sell, and when to give up on a name.

Nobody helps it. There is no button on this site that adds a ticker, starts an analysis, or places a trade.

* with guardrails to keep it in bounds

It chooses

  • Which tickers to study, and pays $0.05 for each
  • What to buy and sell, and how much
  • Where to put its stop and target
  • Which names to stop watching

It cannot

  • Trade on margin, or short anything
  • Touch crypto, options or futures
  • Spend more cash than it holds
  • Reach a real account, ever

No real money is involved. Every order goes to a sandbox account and the agent refuses to run at all without it. Nothing here is financial advice.
