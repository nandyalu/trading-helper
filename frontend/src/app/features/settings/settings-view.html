Trade horizon

Sets what the analysis aims for, how long to wait before grading a signal, and how far price may drift before a Hold counts as wrong. Signals already recorded keep the horizon they were made under.

Analysis model

The LLM every analysis runs on. Each signal records the model it was made with, so the scorecard's "By model" table splits the win rates, which is the only honest way to compare two models. A model with a small context window may fail partway through an analysis rather than answer badly — check the logs after switching.

Intraday alerts

The watchdog scans every 15 minutes during US market hours.

Auto trader

Lets the model trade a simulated account on its own, each weekday just after the US open. It decides what to buy and how much; the budget is a hard limit it cannot exceed. Orders only ever reach the simulated account — see Auto trader.

The conviction floor: how good a signal has to look before the agent may open a position on it. Zero means no floor, and that is the default on purpose — the chance of working is the model's own claim, so check the Scorecard first. A threshold on a number that has not earned trust feels like discipline while being arbitrary. Selling is never blocked by this, and a signal that states no number counts as failing rather than passing.
