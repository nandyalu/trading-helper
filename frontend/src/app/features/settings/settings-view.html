Trade horizon

Sets what the analysis aims for, how long to wait before grading a signal, and how far price may drift before a Hold counts as wrong. Signals already recorded keep the horizon they were made under.

Analysis model

The LLM every analysis runs on. Each signal records the model it was made with, so the scorecard's "By model" table shows how a new one compares before you trust it. A model with a small context window may fail partway through an analysis rather than answer badly — check the logs after switching.

Position sizing

Risk % bounds the loss if a stop is hit. The position cap bounds concentration: a low-volatility stock produces a large share count from a small risk budget, so without a cap one name can take half the account.

Paper trading

Only used when no account equity is set. With equity configured, paper buys use the same risk sizing as the real book, so the paper equity curve reflects what your account would have done.

Intraday alerts

The watchdog scans every 15 minutes during US market hours.

Auto trader

Lets the model trade a simulated account on its own, each weekday just after the US open. It decides what to buy and how much; the budget is a hard limit it cannot exceed. Orders only ever reach the simulated account — see Auto trader.

Webull
